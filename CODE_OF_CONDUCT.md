# Code of Conduct

jear is for everyone. We are building a Jev-routed client for NEAR AI Cloud and IronClaw that anyone can use and trust.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation welcoming regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender, experience level, nationality, or views.

We pledge to move slowly, review kindly, and build greatness together.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and respectful in issues, PRs, and chats.
- Give actionable feedback: what changed, why it matters, how to verify.
- Keep it small: one file at a time, tests included.
- Protect privacy: no secrets, no private data, TEE-first for sensitive work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or secrets without consent.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Any conduct that would make others feel unsafe using or contributing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, then temporarily or permanently remove contributors who violate this code. Reports are kept private.

Report: open a private security advisory or email the maintainers listed in `SECURITY.md`.
